# Patch Refinement Plan

This document outlines the plan to refine all 64 Polysonix ROM patches to achieve a global score of 9+.

## Global Objectives
- **Increase Expression**: Ensure all patches respond musically to Velocity (Filter, Amp) and ModWheel (Vibrato, Timbre). Target is to have Velocity mapped to Filter Cutoff or Mod C (Timbre) in most patches.
- **Boost Creativity**: Use unconventional modulation, interesting waveforms (Banks 12-15), and effects (Bitcrush, RingMod) where appropriate.
- **Enhance Completeness**: Refine envelopes, ensure consistent volume levels, and polish the final sound.
